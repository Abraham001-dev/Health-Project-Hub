Executive Director – Her Health Project Hub

Every visionary leader begins with a story—and for the Executive Director of Her Health Project Hub, that story is shaped by more than a decade of dedicated service in medicine and public health.

Dr. Hadiza Magaji Mahmoud is a medical doctor and public health practitioner whose career began on the front lines of healthcare delivery in Northern Nigeria. She served women, children, and families across Kano, Katsina, and Jigawa States, working in Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Paediatrics, and General Medicine.

From busy delivery wards to fragile neonatal units and complex medical cases, she witnessed both the remarkable resilience of patients and the systemic gaps within Nigeria’s healthcare system. These experiences instilled in her a deep commitment to equity, dignity, and meaningful health reform.

Dr. Hadiza earned her MBBS and a Master’s degree in Reproductive Health from Bayero University, Kano, and later obtained an MSc in Public Health from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine, University of London.

Her work spans clinical practice, teaching hospitals, public health research, and global health engagement. She has participated in international conferences across Dubai, Europe, and the United States, and is a member of leading professional bodies including the UK Faculty of Public Health, FSRH, the Royal Society of Public Health, and EUPHA.

Throughout her career, she encountered the real-life challenges faced by women and girls—unsafe childbirth, unmet menstrual health needs, infertility stigma, and lack of support for survivors of gender-based violence.

These lived realities inspired the creation of Her Health Project Hub—an integrated platform dedicated to care, advocacy, research, and empowerment for women’s health in Nigeria.

Today, Dr. Hadiza leads the organization with compassion, clarity, and evidence-driven expertise, bridging research and action, local realities and global best practices.

Dr. Hadiza Magaji Mahmoud is a resident doctor in the Department of Community Medicine at Aminu Kano Teaching Hospital and the Founder and Executive Director of Her Health Project Hub. Her leadership reflects a steadfast belief: when women’s health is prioritized, entire communities thrive.
